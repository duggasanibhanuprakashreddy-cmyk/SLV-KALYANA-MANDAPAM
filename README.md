1. **Light Luxury Aesthetics (Inspired by KM Palace)**:
   - Ivory (`#FAF7F2`), champagne cream, and royal gold palettes.
   - Traditional auspicious motifs (Sudarshana Chakra, Shankha, Lord Venkateswara Swamy blessings).
   - Animated fairy lights garland hanging across the top header.
2. **Interactive 3D Lighting & Ambience Studio**:
   - Presets: *Twilight Royal Glow*, *Grand Gold Mandap*, and *Daylight Elegance*.
   - Interactive visualizer hotspots for roof lighting, main hall, generator, and road frontage.
3. **Interactive Subha Muhurtham & Availability Calendar**:
   - Month-by-month calendar highlighting auspicious Telugu marriage dates (⭐ Subha Muhurthams).
   - Slot selection (Morning Muhurtham, Evening Reception, 24-Hr Full Day).
   - Direct 1-click WhatsApp date inquiry & booking form prefill.

   - Edited, watermark-free photos of Exterior, Main Hall, Seated Gathering, Dining Hall, Mandap Stage, Feast, and Decor.
   - Fullscreen lightbox with captioning and keyboard navigation (← / → / Esc).
5. **Interactive Tariff & Package Calculator**:
   - Real-time estimates based on event type, guest count, and rooms with 1-click WhatsApp dispatch.
6. **Location Hub, GPS Map & QR Code Scanner**:
   - High-res QR code scanner (`assets/images/location_qr.png`) for instant mobile camera navigation.
   - Embedded Google Map for Thamballapalle (Plus Code: `RF92+82`).
